# Execution-change list

**Version anchor.** The manuscript sources, the code and the result artifacts in this repository correspond to commit `a56a7f7`. The sentence in the manuscript's data-availability statement that points here resolves to that commit. Commits after it change only this anchor and files regenerated by the release-packaging step.

This is the append-only record of decisions taken **during** execution that were not fixed by `design-plan.md`, plus the values that were settled at execution time. Nothing here changes a pre-specified confirmatory parameter (SESOI, confirmatory family, class universe, backbone pair); those were frozen before the confirmatory results were inspected.

## 1. Interpretation rulings

| id | conflict | ruling |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| J1 | what "equal `n_c`" means for A1 | equality is imposed on the C-side recording count; both A0 and A1 use the full C side |
| J2 | Q-draw noise versus the 2% tolerance | capacity balancing: over 400 random subsets, keep the one minimising `abs(sum(floor(k/2)) - budget)` |
| J3 | the ±2% tolerance applied to α | moved to the `n_c` side (`A1_n_c_side_match`); α is only gated on "realised pool ≥ qp_p5" and is disclosed per class |
| J5 | `m_c = 0.60·S_c` does not fit on the C side | cap at the C side and record `m_c_capped` explicitly (no class triggered the cap in this batch) |
| J6/J7 | small classes cannot reach the α target | structurally infeasible classes are listed separately as `alpha_infeasible` (35 classes) and are not mixed into hard failures |
| P3-9.1 | probe evaluation set | probe is evaluated on an 8:2 split of the *training* side; on instrument-held-out folds the probe is undefined |
| P3-9.3 | adversarial strength | λ capped at 0.02 with a two-epoch warm-up; λ = 0.1 is reported as a representation collapse, not as a tuning result |

## 2. Values settled at execution time

Three values were fixed while the pipeline was being built. The ones listed here are the ones the manuscript and the artifacts use:

1. the α tolerance is implemented on the `n_c` side rather than as a hard gate on α (see J3);
2. the per-class counts in the cross-instrument section are 191 and 167;
3. the GZ_IsoTech slice-count distribution is `{1: 2778, 2: 26, 3: 11, 4: 9}`.

## 3. Negative controls

Six control contrasts (three libraries × two backbones) were run at seed 0. Five had a 95% CI spanning zero. The single exception (chest_falsetto, Swin-T, +5.57) was re-run at three seeds and moved to −2.53 [−4.97, −0.19] with a 16.12-point seed spread. The manuscript therefore reports "five of six intervals span zero, and the sixth returns to within the interval once three seeds are used", and states that chest_falsetto has only four classes, 1,280 slices and 257 test slices, so a single-seed Δ carries no discriminating power there.

## 4. Cross-instrument revisions

Two changes were made to the cross-instrument stage after the first run:

* the probe was moved to the training side (§1, P3-9.1), because a probe evaluated on an instrument-held-out fold has no defined notion of "seen instrument";
* the adversarial term was capped at λ = 0.02 with a warm-up (P3-9.3), after λ = 0.1 collapsed the representation (pooled score 0.2080). No third λ was tried.

Both changes are disclosed in the manuscript, together with the probe AUC/top-1 values, so that the negative result on the adversarial term can be read as "the mechanism did not take effect" rather than "the mechanism took effect and did not help".

## 5. Things deliberately not claimed

* No study-wide GPU-hour total is claimed: timing comes from integer-minute driver logs that are incomplete and rounded.
* No claim is made about the audited authors' actual split procedure; the audit reconstructs one slice-level reading of the stated ratios.
* A2's self-test score is illustrative, not a reproduction of the original benchmark score.

## 6. Prose-to-artifact binding

The number verifier checks that every printed value exists somewhere in the
manuscript; it cannot check that a value is attached to the right qualifier.
One revision failed exactly that way, printing the bel canto recording-level
baseline as "low 0.9s" while the artifact cell held 0.83, so a second gate was
added. `data/results/claim_ledger.csv` binds 88 quantitative prose claims to the
cell they come from, and `code/verify_claim_provenance.py` fails if the cell no
longer evaluates to the printed value, if the phrase leaves its section, or if
the qualifier leaves the sentence. `code/negtest_claim_provenance.py` reverts
three wordings in a temporary copy and requires the expected claims to fail, so
the gate is checked against its own failure modes.

Building the ledger exposed one artifact gap. The ten rows of the headroom table
existed only in the manuscript source, with no artifact behind them;
`code/headroom_table.py` now re-derives them from `e1_metrics.csv` (mean of the
three A0 seeds) and `e1_delta.csv`, and the ten re-derived rows match the table.

One pair of numbers is worth stating explicitly, because the manuscript prints
both and they differ by one. Section 5 describes the budget: remove 4,177 slices
and replace them classwise with 1,665 slices from unused C content and 2,512
from R, which sums to 4,177. Supplement Table 1 reports the realised count:
4,178 pool slices are actually present in A1 training, which is the row count of
`data/splits/v2/CTIS_qpool.csv`. The two figures answer different questions (what
the per-class budget allocates, and what the realised pool contains), and the
packaging manifest quotes the second. The companion counts in the same table,
753 injected recordings and 578 of 963 exposed test recordings, were recomputed
from `CTIS_qpool.csv` and `CTIS_qtest.csv` and match.

## 7. Corrections made in response to human review

A reader report on the submitted draft raised 25 points. Nineteen were accepted
as stated, five were accepted with a correction to the premise, one was accepted
in part, and one was rejected with evidence; the assessment and the disposition
of each point are recorded in the project tree under
`report/审稿意见/human-2026-11-12/`. The changes that touch a reported number or
a factual attribution are listed here.

* **Abstract.** The journal requires a structured abstract of 150--250 words.
  The submitted draft was 338 words and had no structure; it is now 239 words in
  four labelled parts, with no change to any reported value.
* **Slice count in CTIS.** Section 4 now states both counts and why they differ:
  the minimum-recording rule leaves 42,509 of the 43,054 released slices in 201
  classes, four of those slices repeat a content hash already claimed by the
  sealed panel because the same take is released under two technique names, and
  the partitions built from them carry 42,505. Table 1 reports the partition
  counts, and its caption says so. The 42,505 is the sum of
  `data/splits/v2/CTIS_{c,r,qtest,qpool}.csv`, not an error.
* **FMA split unit.** The submitted draft called the FMA partition
  recording-level. It is artist-level: the release states that an artist filter
  keeps every artist in one set only. Section 2 now says artist filter.
* **Sturm attribution.** Section 2 attributes to Sturm the documented mislabels,
  duplicate takes and the inflated accuracy they produce, and no longer the
  stronger claim that the benchmark's conclusions change.
* **Interval stream.** Table 3 and the class-set comparison in the supplement
  re-draw 2,000 recording-cluster replicates on two independently implemented
  streams, so their bounds differ in the second decimal while the point
  estimates are identical. Both captions now say this instead of implying one
  shared stream.
* **Units.** Figures 1 and 2 and Table 3 state their scale: macro-F1 on 0--1,
  contrasts and seed ranges in percentage points. Figure 2 previously plotted
  macro-F1 multiplied by 100 while Figure 4 plotted the same quantity on 0--1.
* **Partition names.** Supplement Section 1.11 now maps its arm labels onto the
  partition names of Section 5.7 (A0 = R-slice, A1 and A2 = L-inst,
  A1r = R-rec), and the cross-instrument paragraph of Section 6.4 names the
  partition whose contrast it reports.
* **Wording of the change log.** The manuscript said "during revision", which
  implies a publicly available earlier version. It now says "after the main
  runs", and the same wording is used in the supplement.

The three gates were re-run after these edits: 338 number checks, 88 ledger rows
and the four negative-test cases all pass, and the two figures were regenerated
from the same artifacts.
